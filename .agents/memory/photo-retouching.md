---
name: Photo retouching
description: Preserving the owners' appearance during background cleanup.
---
For background-only cleanup of the owners' photo, preserve the original people and composition rather than replacing the entire photo with an AI rendition.

**Why:** Image editing can change framing and facial details even when explicitly asked not to. A background cleanup output changed a landscape photo to square.

**How to apply:** Inspect the output dimensions and appearance before using it. If necessary, composite only cleaned background regions onto the original, checking coordinates against actual image dimensions rather than a resized preview.
